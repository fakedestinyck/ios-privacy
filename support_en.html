Before you write in

Including your iOS version, device model, and the app version (shown at the bottom of Settings inside the app) makes it much faster to work out what happened.

The app can't open my video

Very long recordings and unusual codecs can take a while or fail outright. Try a shorter clip first to see whether the file itself is the problem.

Export failed partway through

Exporting needs free storage roughly the size of the finished video. Check available space, then try again with the app in the foreground.

Where do my files go?

Finished videos are saved to your photo library. Nothing is uploaded — see the privacy policy.
