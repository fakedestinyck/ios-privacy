Fancam Generator · Privacy Policy · 中文

Nothing leaves your phone.

Fancam Generator does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. There is no account, no server, and no analytics.

What the app does with your videos

All processing happens on your device. The videos and photos you open in the app are read locally, edited locally, and saved locally. Nothing is uploaded anywhere, and we never see them.

Photo library access

The app asks for permission to your photo library so you can pick a video to work on and save the result. That access is used only while you are using the app, and only for those two purposes.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no third-party tracking SDKs. No data is shared with anyone, because none is gathered in the first place.

Children

Because the app collects no data at all, it collects no data from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
